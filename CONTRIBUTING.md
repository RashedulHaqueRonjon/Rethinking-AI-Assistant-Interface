# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in *Rethinking the AI Assistant Interface and Interaction*. This is an open proposal, and feedback makes it better.

## Ways to contribute

- **Give feedback on the ideas.** Open an issue or start a discussion: what works, what does not, what is missing. Concrete scenarios and counter-examples are the most useful.
- **Share evidence.** Real examples (with permission and without confidential data) of interfaces that already do something similar, or that fail in the ways the proposal describes.
- **Fix and clarify.** Pull requests for typos, broken links, unclear wording and figure labels are welcome.
- **Build and test.** Prototypes of any part of the proposal (Figma, HTML, Android, web) and findings from trying them with users.
- **Translate.** Translations are welcome. Keep the original structure and numbering.

## How the document is maintained

The main document (points 1 to 23) and its key statements are maintained by the author, so that the proposal stays coherent. Substantive changes are discussed first in an issue or discussion. Ideas that extend or challenge the proposal can live in their own files next to it (for example in `docs/extensions/`) and link back to the point they relate to.

## Pull request checklist

- One logical change per pull request.
- Keep figure numbers and cross-references consistent. Figures are numbered like the points they illustrate.
- Do not add confidential or personal data, or screenshots you do not have the right to share.
- By contributing you agree that your contribution is licensed under CC BY 4.0.

## Be kind

Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas, not people. Harassment and personal attacks are not tolerated.

## Questions

Open a discussion or an issue in this repository.
